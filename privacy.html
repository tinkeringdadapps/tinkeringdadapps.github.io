Privacy

This applies to Ink, Pause, and any other app from Tinkering Dad Apps.

This app does not collect or transmit any data automatically. All usage information stays on your device.

If you choose to send feedback, you can include a usage summary in the email. It's usage data, not content data: which features you've opened and how often, but never what you're actually doing with them, like which sites or apps you've chosen to pause or your reasons for continuing past a pause.

You decide whether to include the summary, and because it's written right into the email, you'll see exactly what's being sent before you hit send.
