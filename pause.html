Pause

A moment to think before you open the apps and sites that pull you in.

When you open something you've chosen to pause, Pause shows a short countdown and asks why you're going in. You can still continue. History shows how often you reach for the apps you've paused, by day, week, or month, so you can see what's changing.

Pause doesn't block anything or keep score. It just brings the choice back to you.
