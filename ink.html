Ink

A calm home screen for your phone. It looks like e-ink and works like the navigation you already know.

Ink softens the pull of your phone without adding friction. Everything stays easy to reach, but the screen stops competing for your attention. People should keep using it because it's pleasant, not because they're trying to cut their screen time.
