Install and help

These apps aren't in the Play store yet, so you install them from a download link. It's safe, and it takes about two minutes.

Install steps

  1. On your Android phone, open the page for Ink or Pause and tap the download button.
  2. On the download page, find the newest version at the top. Under "Assets," tap the file ending in .apk.
  3. Your browser may warn that this type of file can be harmful. That's normal for any app installed outside the Play store. Tap Download anyway.
  4. Open the downloaded file. If Android says installs from this source aren't allowed, tap Settings, turn on "Allow from this source," then go back.
  5. Tap Install. If Play Protect says it hasn't seen the app before, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  6. Open the app and follow the setup prompts.

Updating

Download the newer file the same way and install it over the old one. Your data stays.

Common questions

Is it safe to install?
The warnings appear for every app that doesn't come from the Play store. These apps are made by a person you know, and the privacy page explains exactly what they do with data.
Does the app send my data anywhere?
No. Nothing is sent automatically. If you choose to send feedback, you decide whether to include a usage summary, and you can read it in the email before sending. See privacy.
How do I send feedback?
Tap the feedback button inside the app. Your email app opens with a message ready to write. You can also email tinkeringdadapps@gmail.com.
Something didn't install or work. What now?
Send a note with your phone model and what happened. Screenshots help.
How do I remove an app?
Remove it like any other app: press and hold its icon, then tap Uninstall.

Questions or ideas? Email tinkeringdadapps@gmail.com or use the feedback button inside each app.
